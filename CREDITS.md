# Credits

This template adapts ideas and structure from these open-source projects. All of them are MIT-licensed; their copyright notices are reproduced below.

| Source | What we took |
|--------|--------------|
| [bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD](https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD) `skills/bmad-prd` | Brain-dump-first discovery, stakes calibration, Fast vs. Coaching modes, "elicitation, not direction", named-protagonist user journeys, essential spine + adapt-in section menu, `[ASSUMPTION]` tagging, seven-dimension quality rubric, reviewer-as-subagent pattern |
| [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) `grilling` | Design-tree / frontier interviewing in numbered rounds with recommended answers; "yes accepts the recommendation"; "facts are Claude's job, decisions are the user's" |
| [bartr/ai](https://github.com/bartr/ai) `prd-simple` | Phased question bank, resumable session state, fill gaps with explicit assumptions instead of blocking |
| [NickCrew/Claude-Cortex](https://github.com/NickCrew/Claude-Cortex) `requirements-discovery` | Open vs. closed question patterns, interview anti-patterns, MoSCoW scoping, PRD quality checklist |

Also reviewed: [pmprompt/claude-plugin-product-management](https://github.com/pmprompt/claude-plugin-product-management) `prd-writer`. It's a one-shot generator with no interview, so we didn't adopt it.
